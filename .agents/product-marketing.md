## Product Overview
**One-liner (draft):** A three-generation, family-run, DAMAS-accredited dental lab in Christchurch, Dorset, making removable appliances (retainers, night guards, aligners, dentures and more) for UK practices since 1978. Turnaround is 3, 5 or 7 working days depending on the appliance, and the lab accepts STL files from all major intraoral scanners.
**What it does:** Makes custom removable appliances, models and dentures to a practice's prescription, from impressions or STL scan files. Practices within 5 miles get the lab's own collection service; everywhere else in the UK sends cases by Royal Mail. [Sam]
**Product category (the "shelf"):** Dental laboratory, specialising in **removable appliances and orthodontic work** (retainers, night guards/bruxism splints, clear aligners, Essix, snore guards, whitening trays, immediate dentures). **This is not a crown and bridge or implant lab.** Those services weren't listed, so crown and bridge and implant ad groups would advertise services the lab doesn't offer. [TBC: Sam to confirm the lab doesn't do crown and bridge or implants]
**Product type:** B2B manufacturing service (custom-made dental devices, to prescription).
**Business model:** Per-case fees invoiced to the practice. Practices pay Royal Mail postage themselves. [Sam] [TBC: price list; whether prices can appear in ads; invoicing terms]

**Customer:** a UK dental practice (target: private). **Conversion:** a practice sends its first case. **Churn:** a practice with **no orders for more than 3 months** counts as inactive. [Sam]

### Warranty / remakes [Site: warranty page, pasted by Sam 2026-11-04; URL TBC]
**Heading on page:** "Every appliance, covered. We stand behind our workmanship."
**Period varies by appliance**, runs from the date of delivery, subject to fair wear and tear. The only period in the pasted text is **hard nightguard: 6 months from delivery**.
> ⚠ **Conflict:** Sam's Step 1 answer said a **3-month** warranty, but the site shows **6 months** for the hard nightguard. No ad or page may state a warranty period until Sam confirms the period for each appliance. [TBC: warranty period for each appliance on the page's list]

**Covered:** genuine making fault (poor fit, wrong specification); genuine material failure confirmed on inspection; claim reported within the period with the appliance returned.
**Not covered:** patient-caused breakage (misuse, heat, dropping, biting in, pets); loss or non-returned appliances; patient symptoms, comfort or clinical outcomes; changes to the patient's teeth after fitting; normal wear and tear; staining; denture repairs and additions (at the lab's discretion).
**Voids cover:** heat and hot water, warm storage, abrasive or harsh cleaning, uneven force putting in or taking out, forcing an ill-fitting retainer, whitening gel left on, any adjustment by anyone other than the lab, damage, pets, loss.
**Claims and remakes (published):**
| Case | Charge |
|---|---|
| Lab's fault (making fault or material failure) | **No charge.** Appliance must come back for inspection, with a new scan or impression |
| Patient breakage | **Half price with a photo, full price if nothing is sent.** One remake per order at the same model |
| Breaks again | Upgrade to a harder-wearing model: Essix → Zendura A → Zendura FLX; soft → soft-hard → hard (Michigan) |
| Remake from a model or scan on file | Practice confirms it still matches the patient's teeth; if it doesn't, the remake is chargeable |

> ⚠ **Small inconsistency on the page:** "send the appliance back — or a clear photo" vs "we must have the appliance back to inspect." This is fine if the photo route applies only to patient breakage, but the wording should say so. (Finding for Step 2.)

**Why this matters for marketing:** published remake rules are real evidence of transparent pricing. They support a "clear terms, no surprises" angle (manager persona) and the upgrade path supports "we'll fix the right thing, not the same thing" (owner persona). They don't by themselves prove "no hidden fees", which still needs the price list.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** UK **private** dental practices, plus orthodontic and aligner-providing practices. **Hypothesis:** the retainer and night guard volume suggests orthodontic, aligner, cosmetic and general private practices are the best fit. Current mix is about 90% private and the goal is more private work. [Sam]
**Decision-makers:** Practice owner / principal dentist; practice manager. [Brief]
**Geography:** UK-wide [Brief]. This works because cases come in as STL uploads or by Royal Mail. The local collection service is a bonus for practices within 5 miles, not the main offer.
**Primary use case:** A dependable lab for retainers and night guards, with short, stated turnaround and digital intake from the practice's existing scanner.
**Jobs to be done (hypotheses):**
- "Get a retainer back quickly after debond or aligner treatment, without the patient waiting weeks." (owner)
- "Send night guard and retainer scans from our scanner to a lab we can rely on." (owner/manager)
- "Know what we'll pay: no surprise extras on the invoice." (manager; from churn-back reasons below)

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Cheaper labs look good on price but cost more in remakes, poor fit and extra charges. [Sam: practices that leave "because someone was cheaper" come back "because the quality was poor or because of hidden fees."]
**What it costs them:** Remake appointments, chair time, patient goodwill, invoice surprises.
**Emotional tension:** Owner: "Will this embarrass me in front of my patient?" Manager: "What will the invoice actually say?"

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Remakes, poor fit and hidden fees at cheaper labs; slow retainer turnaround.
**Pull:** 3, 5 or 7 working days; STL from their scanner; three-generation family lab; DAMAS/ISO; warranty.
**Habit:** Existing lab already set up in the scanner portal; prescription forms; postage routine.
**Anxiety:** First case going wrong; setting up a new portal connection; postage cost and risk.
