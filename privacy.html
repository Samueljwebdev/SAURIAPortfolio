Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not store anything on your device. It uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and which buttons are pressed. That tool is cookieless and does not build a profile of you or follow you across other sites. It records things like the page visited, country and browser type. Because nothing is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner to accept.

The page loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

If you send me your page

The form on this site does not send data to me by itself. It opens your own email app with a message ready to send, and nothing reaches me until you press send. What arrives is your email address, the page address you gave me and anything you add. I use it only to reply, to send the three fixes, and to talk about work if you ask. Lawful basis: your request, and my legitimate interest in answering a business enquiry. I keep enquiry emails for up to 2 years, or longer where we become client and need records, and tax records for 6 years.

If I emailed you first

I send a small number of individually written emails to limited companies whose websites I have looked at. I get business contact details from the company's own website, from public registers such as Companies House, and from email-finding tools. I use them only to write to that business about its website. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, because the email is relevant to the company's own business. I keep your details for up to 12 months, or until you tell me to stop. Reply "stop" and I will delete them and will not contact you again.

Who else handles data

Google Workspace (email), Vercel (website hosting and analytics) and, for finding business contact details, Hunter. Each acts on my instructions. Some of these providers process data outside the UK under standard safeguards, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.
