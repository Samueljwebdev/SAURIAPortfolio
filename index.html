Method

Six jobs go into the one page.

Each one has a way of being checked. Where I have a real number, it is here. Where I only have a method, I say so.

01 / Enquiry path

Make the next step obvious

One job per page, one button, a short form that asks what you need to quote. Phone number and form within reach on a phone.

Result: at the retailer above, orders per 100 search visitors went from 3.2 to 4.0 in August, year on year.

02 / Copywriting

Say what you do in the first line

Welcome to Smith & Sons Ltd
Fitted kitchens, made in our Leeds workshop. Quote in 48 hours.

Why it matters: 45 of the 100 sites I checked had no main heading. The example is made up to show the change.

03 / Copy editing

Cut what a customer would skip

We pride ourselves on delivering bespoke solutions of the highest quality.
Every staircase is measured, made and fitted by the same two joiners.

Check: every claim on your page gets a fact, a photo or a name behind it, or it goes.

04 / Search audit

Get found for the job you want

Page title, description, headings, internal links and speed, set for the service and the place you want work from.

Result: +111% search visitors and 16 to 42 orders in August, year on year, at the retailer above.

05 / Schema

Tell Google what the page is

Structured data for your business, your service and your common questions, so search engines and AI assistants can read the page properly.

Check it yourself: this page carries the same markup. Run Google's test on it.

06 / Enquiry tracking

Count what the page brings in

Form sends, phone taps and email clicks are counted from launch day. You get the 30 day number in writing.

Check: the count is yours, good or bad. It is how you judge whether the page paid for itself.
